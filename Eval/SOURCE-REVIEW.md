# KITT emergency guide source review

This is a machine-assisted editorial review of the 20 entries in guide pack v5 and the changes proposed in v6 and its v7 correction. It covers the watch answers, conditional notes, and the passages used by the optional model. Every entry retains its pending human review status. The reviewed build uses the guide and calculator by default. Model fine-tuning and physical-watch validation were outside the measurements below.

## Evaluation

Read the trial transcripts alongside the counts. A direct match means the first reply cites the expected entry. Offered means it appears as a button. Neither measure alone establishes the answer is medically correct.

The first trial fixed one question per topic before edits. Across 20 questions, v5 got 8 direct, 11 offered and 1 missed. The initial v6 revision got 9 direct, 10 offered and 1 missed. Neither produced an outright wrong topic on those prompts. The complete miss in both builds involved treating water when boiling was unavailable. That question was then moved into development and used to fix retrieval. The first-trial results are kept as measured before that fix. [First trial](trials/source-review-v6.json)

The six additional confirmation questions were fixed before the targeted water edit. See the paired outputs and the new regression family. These small trials were authored in the same session as the changes. Independently written questions, mixed hazards and unsupported topics are still needed. [Water confirmation](trials/source-review-water.json)

The existing development suite checks actions and flags. A new opening-sentence check also fails a reply that gives the requested emergency action only after another sentence. The six clinical and gas regressions and the water-treatment regression are development cases. Training and validation examples for future model experiments are separate from both trials. [Training examples](../Training/README.md)

The second set went from 2 direct, 2 offered and 2 misses to 2 direct, 3 offered and 1 miss. The gasoline question continued to get the contamination warning directly. The remaining missed control saved the household details but did not return the requested water total. The exact inability-to-boil question from the first set now passes as a development regression.

The final Swift core run passed 66 tests on macOS. On the combined development and historical held-out suite, the model-off pipeline passed 280 of 290 answer-text checks and all 199 flag checks. The baseline was 272 of 283 answer checks. Seven development cases were added and one outdated drinking-quantity expectation was replaced, so the two suite percentages should not be treated as a like-for-like comparison. The zero critical failures is the scorer's result on these cases only.

## Corrections to patch 03 (guide pack v7)

The rationing answer restores FEMA’s exception for an order from local authorities. The dehydration answer restores adult symptoms and credits the American Red Cross instructor’s manual for the swallowing precaution. The watch source screen lists each publication used by an entry. The heat answer keeps the 911 instruction first and restores the temperature and skin signs. Both versions of each passage are synchronized. [FEMA basic preparedness](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/areyouready/basic_preparedness.pdf) · [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/dehydration.html) · [American Red Cross manual](https://www.redcross.org/content/dam/redcross/delta/EMR_InstructorsManual_2017_LoRes_111017.pdf) · [CDC heat illness sheet](https://www.cdc.gov/extreme-heat/media/pdf/Heat_Related_Illness.pdf)

The freezer example now requires the conditional frozen-insulin warning in the first sentence. A report that a pen was in a freezer does not establish that its contents froze. Both heat-preparation checks now reject the actual 911 opening used by the heat-illness entry. These three revised expectations are regression checks on previously seen questions. The v5/v6 trial transcripts above are historical measurements.

On macOS, 67 core tests passed with v7. The model-off suite passed 280 of 290 answer checks and 199 of 199 flag checks. Feeding the actual heat-stroke answer to the two preparedness expectations failed both as intended. The Series 5 executable linked for arm64_32. On replay of the 20-question review trial, 10 received a direct answer and 10 offered the topic. The six water questions produced two direct answers, three offers, and one miss. These questions had already been seen. All 14 stop-and-guide conversations replayed with their expected household durations. The two requests to change the seven-day plan produced five days, and the other 12 preserved seven days.
